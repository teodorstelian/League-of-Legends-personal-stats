How ban priority works

Raw priority = 70% loss rate + 30% (100 − your average Rift Score). Adjusted priority = 50 + (raw − 50) × games / (games + 5). This pulls smaller samples toward neutral. Only adjusted values above 50 are suggested; the list sorts highest first. Ban priority is a custom ranking, not a win probability or Rift Score. Games across your selected champion pool are pooled against each opponent. Fewer than 10 games is labeled limited evidence. No pick-rate or ban-rate data is used.
