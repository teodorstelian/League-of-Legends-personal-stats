About these stats

P10/P90 mark the lower/upper score percentiles in each sample. Spread is population standard deviation. Review flags require at least 10 scored games and a mean at least 10 points from your overall mean. Groups under 10 scored games have limited evidence. Falling below the review threshold does not establish fairness.

Component diagnostics

Mean / 100 measures the component alone; mean score points measures its weighted contribution. Frequent 100s can indicate an easy target; frequent zeroes can indicate a hard target or absent activity. Neither proves bias.
