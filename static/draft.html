How recommendations & filters work

Personal ranked history, not global counters or a win prediction. Role applies to picks, lane bans, and matchup history; general bans span all roles. Queue and period apply throughout. Already-selected enemies are excluded from your picks and bans. Only saved data is used; no live-client access or extra Riot import is triggered.

All recorded matchups are visible. Recommendations with fewer than 3 games are labeled limited data and rank after supported candidates.

Lane and general candidates ranked together. Qualified evidence comes first; limited-data candidates fill remaining spaces and are labeled. Each champion appears once, using its highest-ranked evidence. Lane evidence follows your role; general evidence spans all your roles. Queue and period apply to both.

Same-role assignments are not verified lane duels. Win rate is match outcome; score and K/D/A are yours. Ambiguous or missing opponents are excluded.

How the shortlist is ranked

Comfort index: 80% adjusted average Rift Score + 20% adjusted win rate. Each value is shrunk toward 50 using n / (n + 5), where n is its sample size. With enough same-role games against the selected enemy, matchup ranking combines 60% comfort index + 40% matchup index. Otherwise we fall back to comfort picks. These indices only rank the cards; displayed gauges are your actual average Rift Scores. Ban priority uses the formula below. Form is descriptive and is not added to the index. Match outcomes can reflect teammates, champion mix, patches, and skill changes.

How ban priority works

Raw priority = 70% loss rate + 30% (100 − your average Rift Score). Adjusted priority = 50 + (raw − 50) × games / (games + 5). This pulls smaller samples toward neutral. Only adjusted values above 50 are suggested; the list sorts highest first. Ban priority is a custom ranking, not a win probability or Rift Score. Lane evidence pools all your champions in the selected role against each same-role opponent. General evidence pools matches against each enemy-team champion across all your roles. Each match counts once per enemy champion. Evidence size affects confidence, not which matches contribute to a candidate’s score. Fewer than 10 games is labeled limited evidence. No pick-rate or ban-rate data is used.
